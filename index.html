Fibreglass vs Marbelite

Not sure which finish you need?

Quick version: if your pool leaks, cracks or the plaster is failing, fibreglass lining is the long-term fix. If the shell is sound and you just want it looking new, re-marbeliting is the cheaper route.

  • Fibreglass lining — waterproof shell, ~10 year life, smooth to the touch, best for leaks and structural cracks.
  • Marbelite — traditional plaster finish, lower upfront cost, needs redoing sooner, no leak repair.

Good To Know

Frequently asked questions

What is fibreglass pool lining and why is it worth it?

It's a smooth, waterproof fibreglass shell we apply over your existing pool surface. It seals leaks, covers cracks and rough plaster, resists algae, and is comfortable underfoot. It's the most durable resurfacing option we offer.

How long does a fibreglass lining last?

Around 10 years with normal care, often longer. It's not maintenance-free, but it holds up far better than plaster and won't develop the same leaks and rough patches over time.

Can fibreglass lining go over any pool?

It works on most concrete, gunite and marbelite pools that are structurally sound. If there's serious structural movement we'll tell you straight at the inspection rather than line over a problem.

How long does a renovation take?

Most re-lining and re-marbeliting jobs run 5 to 10 working days from drain to refill, depending on the size of the pool, the repairs needed, and the weather. New fibreglass installations are usually 1 to 2 weeks.

How do I look after a fibreglass-lined pool?

Keep the water chemistry balanced, brush it occasionally, and don't let it sit empty in full sun. We give every customer a short care sheet on handover and you can always phone us with questions.
